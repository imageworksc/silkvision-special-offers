EVO ICL Referral Rewards

Share your EVO ICL experience and get rewarded.

STAAR Surgical, the manufacturer of EVO ICL lenses, offers a referral program for eligible patients.

If you’ve had EVO ICL surgery and refer a friend who completes the procedure, you could receive a $100 gift card. Your friend could receive a $200 gift card as well.

How Does the EVO ICL Referral Program Work?

When your referred friend completes a qualifying EVO ICL procedure, you may both be eligible for rewards through STAAR Surgical’s patient referral program. Visit the manufacturer’s website for registration instructions, eligibility requirements, and gift card details.
